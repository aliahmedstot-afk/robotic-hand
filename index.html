How it works

Requires a Bluetooth Low Energy robot using the matching ESP32 firmware. This public page only controls a robot that your device can connect to nearby; it cannot control a robot remotely over the internet.

Web Bluetooth needs HTTPS and a supported browser such as Chrome on Android or Chrome/Edge on desktop.
