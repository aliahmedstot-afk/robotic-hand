Bluetooth connection

This page looks for a nearby BLE device named RobotArm. Bluetooth works only in a supported browser over HTTPS, and the robot firmware must use the same service and characteristic IDs as this page.

Values are sent in this order: base, shoulder, elbow, wrist, gripper. A full 360° base turn needs a suitable continuous-rotation mechanism (and the right position feedback); a normal 180° servo cannot make a full turn.

Tip: connect the robot while it is safely supported and keep the claw clear of people and objects while testing.
