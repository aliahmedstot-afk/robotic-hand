USB connection · Arduino Uno

Connect your Arduino Uno to your computer with a USB data cable, then press Connect USB and choose the Arduino serial port.

The page sends seven comma-separated values followed by a new line, at 9600 baud: base, shoulder, elbow, wrist, gripper, reach, forward tilt. Your Arduino sketch must read this format and control the matching motors. The new reach axis needs a real linear actuator or suitable slide mechanism to move the arm forward and backward.

USB serial in a browser requires HTTPS and a browser that supports Web Serial, such as desktop Chrome or Edge. Some phones and browsers do not support it.

Test with the arm safely supported, clear of people and objects. Base rotation is limited to 130°; shoulder, elbow, wrist and gripper are limited to 90° (gripper max 37°); fine tilt ranges from -90° to +90°. Check that your motors and mechanical joints support the selected range.
